# Game-Devlopment
🎮Flute Of Eternia

A retro-style 2D Action Adventure RPG built with *Godot 4* and *GDScript*, developed for a Game Dev Hackathon.  
Guide the hero on a quest to reclaim the *Flute of Eternia*, stolen by the evil witch Malakar, and restore peace to the kingdom of Valoria.  

---

## 🌟 Storyline  

In the kingdom of Valoria, peace was sustained by the *Flute of Eternia, a divine gift of the gods. One night, the flute was stolen by the witch **Malakar*, who twisted its magic to spread chaos.  

The gods grant a single hero *three sacred lives* to recover it. Each rebirth comes at a cost — the world darkens, and the enemies grow stronger.  

Solve puzzles, defeat monsters, and face Malakar in a final battle of light versus shadow. Will you return the flute to *King Nero* and save Valoria?  

---

## 🕹️ Features  

•⁠  ⁠⚔️ *Top-down combat* (sword, bow, bombs, etc.)  
•⁠  ⁠🧩 *Puzzles & interactables* (statue movement, switches, gates)  
•⁠  ⁠👾 *Enemies & Bosses* (slimes, goblins, Malakar the witch)  
•⁠  ⁠❤️ *3-life mechanic* tied into the story  
•⁠  ⁠📦 *Items, inventory, pickups*  
•⁠  ⁠🏰 *Tilemap-based levels* (villages, ruins, dungeons)  
•⁠  ⁠🎨 Retro pixel-art style with smooth animations
